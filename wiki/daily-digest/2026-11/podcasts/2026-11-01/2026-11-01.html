Three weeks of work point the same way: the cheapest gains now come from removing the part of a signal that carries no information, in RL gradients, in auxiliary losses and in the views a model learns from.

Only about 2% of loss is left for architecture. - Platonic Representation Theorem: JEPAs trained apart converge to one representation if views are good. A 109M text model beats 72B LLMs on alignment. - Decision models move into loops: streaming decisions, gates on agent tool calls, and retrieval by navigation all appeared this window.
