### 1. Speculative decoding: hide the drafter's cost

Speculative decoding has a cheap drafter propose several tokens that the target model verifies in one pass, with rejection sampling keeping the output distribution unchanged. The two levers are acceptance (how many draft tokens survive) and draft cost.

- **[JetSpec++](2026-11-01-jetspec-plus-plus.md)** (11-01) pipelines drafting with verification on a second GPU. It predicts the target's hidden states for the block under verification, pre-drafts a block for each likely verification outcome, and refines drafts with Jacobi passes while waiting. Qwen3-8B on two GB200 GPUs: 7.45 times autoregressive and 1.54 times DSpark in BF16 (1,361 tokens per second), 4,117 with NVFP4, at batch size 1.
- **Built-in drafters.** Multi-token-prediction heads double as drafters. [Soft-MTP](../training-finetuning/2026-11-01-soft-multi-token-prediction.md) (11-01) trains them on a cleaner gradient and reports faster speculative decoding from 167M to 1.2B.
- **DLoop** (10-09) loops the draft stage itself.

### 2. Architecture choices are serving choices

The [11-01 architecture ablation](../models-architectures/2026-11-01-architecture-ablations-since-gpt3.md) judged every change at equal vLLM serving cost under an agent workload. GQA, MQA, MLA and sliding windows win only by cutting the KV cache, linear-attention hybrids are 2 to 7 times cheaper to serve, FP8 weights and KV cache cost about 0.1% in bits per byte, and DSA-style sparse attention pays only beyond about 64k context (1.40 times sessions per GPU at 128k).

### 3. Quantized samplers in RL

RL rollouts are increasingly generated by quantized engines. [Score centering](../reinforcement-learning/2026-11-01-score-centering.md) (11-01) makes an INT8-weight, INT4-KV-cache sampler trainable where importance-sampling corrections collapse, which links serving-side quantization directly to RL cost.

### 4. Kernels and systems noted (11-01, Media Zone)

FlashMask-3 (Baidu): O(n) mask storage that survives multi-node context parallelism, up to 3.71 times Megatron's ring context parallelism. ReplaySSM: caching an SSM's inputs instead of its state, reported in production serving for GLM-5.3-Flash and Kimi-K3 and in TensorRT-LLM, vLLM, FlashInfer and SGLang. A fully fused Zip-NeRF written with an AI coding agent trains in 36 minutes at the official quality. NVIDIA published a validated Kubernetes runtime guide for Dynamo.
